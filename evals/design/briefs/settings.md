# Workspace settings for Harbor

Harbor is team chat for logistics companies, used by dispatchers and drivers. Design the settings screen of its desktop app.

- **Audience**: workspace admins at freight and delivery companies, not technical, often updating settings between calls.
- **Platform**: desktop app, one artboard 1440 × 900 px.
- **Must have**:
  - Settings navigation: Profile, Notifications, Workspace (current), Members, Billing, Security.
  - A workspace form: workspace name "Coastline Freight", workspace URL coastline-freight.harbor.chat, default language English (US), time zone Pacific Time (Los Angeles).
  - The support email field showing a validation error: "dispatch@coastline" is not a complete email address.
  - The data region shown as United States and disabled, with a note that it can't be changed after setup.
  - Notification defaults for new members: three switches (direct messages on, mentions on, every message in a channel off).
  - A message confirming the last change was saved.
  - Save and cancel actions; save is disabled until something changes.
  - A danger zone to delete the workspace, which removes 1,284 messages and 37 members' access.
