# Analytics dashboard for Orbitly

Orbitly is product analytics for mobile apps. Design the overview dashboard a product manager opens every morning for their app, Pocketfit, a home-workout app.

- **Audience**: product managers and growth leads who check the last 30 days, spot what changed and share a number in standup.
- **Platform**: desktop web app, one artboard 1440 × 900 px.
- **Must have**:
  - App navigation with Overview (current), Funnels, Retention, Events, Users and Settings, plus the app switcher showing Pocketfit (iOS and Android).
  - A date range control set to "Last 30 days" compared with the previous 30 days.
  - Four key numbers with their change: daily active users 48,210 (+6.4%), day-7 retention 31.2% (−1.8 points), revenue $184,930 (+12.1%), crash-free sessions 99.42% (+0.05 points).
  - A chart of daily active users over the 30 days, with the previous period for comparison.
  - The onboarding funnel: installed 62,400 → created account 41,180 → finished first workout 22,950 → started trial 6,310 → subscribed 2,140.
  - A table of the top 6 events with name, count and change: workout_started, workout_completed, paywall_viewed, trial_started, plan_shared, streak_lost.
  - One alert: Android crash rate rose after release 4.12.0.
