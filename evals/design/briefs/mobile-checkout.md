# Mobile checkout for Fernhill Grocer

Fernhill Grocer delivers groceries from local farms within two hours. Design the checkout screen of its iPhone app.

- **Audience**: busy parents and young professionals who reorder most weeks and want to finish checkout in under a minute, often one-handed.
- **Platform**: iPhone, one artboard 390 × 844 px; the screen may scroll, so make the artboard taller if the content needs it.
- **Must have**:
  - The delivery address: 48 Alder Lane, Apt 3B, Portland, OR 97214, with a way to change it.
  - Delivery slots for today: 4–5 pm (selected), 5–6 pm, 6–7 pm, and 7–8 pm, which is full.
  - The payment method: Visa ending in 4417, with Apple Pay as an alternative.
  - The order: Organic Honeycrisp apples (2 lb, $5.98), Hillside oat milk (2 × $4.49), Sourdough loaf ($6.50).
  - A promo code FIRSTFARM applied, taking $5.00 off.
  - Subtotal $21.46, delivery $3.99, discount −$5.00, tax $1.62, total $22.07.
  - A note field for the driver.
  - The place-order action showing the total.
