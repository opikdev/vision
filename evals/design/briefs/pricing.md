# Pricing page for Quillstack

Quillstack is shared documentation for engineering teams: runbooks, architecture notes and onboarding guides that stay next to the code. Design its pricing page.

- **Audience**: engineering managers and staff engineers at companies of 20 to 500 people, comparing Quillstack with a wiki they already pay for.
- **Platform**: desktop web, one artboard 1440 px wide; make it as tall as the content needs.
- **Must have**:
  - A short headline and one sentence on what every plan includes.
  - A monthly / yearly billing switch; yearly saves 20%.
  - Four plans:
    - Starter: free for up to 5 editors, 3 spaces, 7-day page history.
    - Team: $10 per editor per month billed yearly ($12 monthly), unlimited spaces, 90-day history, GitHub and GitLab sync. This is the plan most teams pick.
    - Business: $20 per editor per month billed yearly ($24 monthly), SSO with Okta or Azure AD, audit log, unlimited history, read-only guests.
    - Enterprise: custom pricing, self-hosting, a named support engineer, 99.95% uptime SLA. Its button contacts sales.
  - A comparison of the plans across 8 features.
  - Four common questions with answers: what counts as an editor, whether readers are free, what happens to docs if a team downgrades, and how the free trial of Team works (14 days, no card).
  - A closing call to start the 14-day trial.
