# Onboarding for Sprout

Sprout reminds people to water, feed and repot their houseplants. Design its first-run onboarding: three screens of its iPhone app.

- **Audience**: people with 3 to 15 houseplants who have lost one or two to forgetting; many are new to plant care.
- **Platform**: iPhone, three artboards of 390 × 844 px, side by side in order.
- **Must have**:
  - Progress through the three steps on every screen, and a way to go back on screens 2 and 3.
  - Screen 1, welcome: what Sprout does in one line, two short benefits (reminders tuned to each plant, a care log with photos), a continue action and a sign-in link for returning users.
  - Screen 2, add your plants: search, and a grid of 8 common plants to pick from (Monstera, Snake plant, Pothos, Fiddle-leaf fig, ZZ plant, Peace lily, Spider plant, Calathea); Monstera and Pothos are selected. Show how many are selected.
  - Screen 3, reminders: the time for reminders (8:00 AM), which days (weekdays selected), and a request to allow notifications with what happens if they decline.
  - A finish action on screen 3 and a skip option on screens 1 and 2.
