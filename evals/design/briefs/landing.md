# Landing page for Brightwell

Brightwell does bookkeeping for independent restaurants: it reads POS sales, supplier invoices and payroll every night and closes the books monthly with a named accountant. Design the top of its marketing site: the hero and the features.

- **Audience**: owners of one to five restaurants who do the books themselves late at night or pay a bookkeeper who is always behind.
- **Platform**: desktop web, one artboard 1440 px wide; make it as tall as the content needs.
- **Must have**:
  - Navigation: Product, Pricing, Customers, Resources, Sign in, and a "Book a demo" action.
  - A hero with a headline, one supporting sentence, the primary action "Book a demo" and a secondary "See pricing", plus a glimpse of the product, such as a monthly profit-and-loss summary for a restaurant.
  - Proof: "Trusted by 1,200 restaurants" with four customer names: Osteria Lupa, Tin Roof Tacos, Marrow & Vine, Pho Saigon Kitchen.
  - Five features: nightly sync with Toast, Square and Clover; supplier invoices scanned from email; prime cost (food + labor) tracked weekly; payroll and tip reports; month-end close by your own accountant within 10 days.
  - One customer quote: "I got my Sunday nights back. I know my food cost every Monday." — Dana Ruiz, owner of Tin Roof Tacos (3 locations).
  - A closing call to action with the demo button.
